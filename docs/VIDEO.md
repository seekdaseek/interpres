## Rebuild

From the saved captures in `video/captures/` (not in git: frames and stems are too heavy):

```
node --env-file=.env scripts/video/build.ts
```

It makes the identifiers slide, the edit list, the render and the checks, then this page. Each step also runs alone: `identifiers-slide.ts`, `edl.ts`, `render.ts`, `checks.ts`, `video-doc.ts`. A new take is `capture.ts --scene A|B|C`, then `assemble.ts` and `transcribe.ts capture` on it.
